# Record [need:record]

Spine: event; time and actor when material; evidence; remaining obligations.
Keep separate any item needed for audit, consent, safety, or handover. Records
do not add stance.

Example:

**Record: landlord call, August 15 at 14:20, both parties.**

- Repairs: agreed for September 2.
- Boiler: added during the call; exact words recorded in the follow-up email.
- Lease extension: discussed, not agreed.
- Evidence: email sent at 14:41; delivery receipt retained.
- Open: contractor date due August 20; absent means follow up.
