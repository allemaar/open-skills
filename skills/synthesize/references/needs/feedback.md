# Feedback [need:feedback]

Spine: maker's intent; what works; what obstructs it; one specific revision.
Use intent language, not defect language, for creative work.

Example:

**Intent:** make the reader care about the river.

**Already works:** the opening image creates that connection. Keep it.

**Obstructs it:** the middle becomes three paragraphs of detached statistics.

**Revision:** retain `4 tons` and `900 meters`; attach each to a person acting.
