# Procedure [need:procedure]

Spine: prerequisites; ordered steps; done-signal per stage; branches; flagged
hazards or one-way steps; recourse. Do not flatten branches into a straight
line.

Example:

**Renewal: about `20 min`; bring the old card and payment method.**

1. Submit the form. Done when the receipt appears.
2. If identity changed, upload proof; otherwise continue.
3. Review the address. **One-way after submission:** corrections require a new
   application.
4. Pay. Done when the confirmation email arrives.
