# Decide [need:decide]

Spine: decision; viable options including real no-action; consequences and
uncertainty; criteria; decision owner; reversibility.

Example:

> **Hold Tuesday:** it is free and preserves both options. Your call by
> `17:00`.

- **Hold:** senior specialist; cancellation stays free until Monday.
- **Wait:** no commitment, but the slot releases and the next is in `3 weeks`.
- **Unknown:** insurer approval is due Monday; a refusal flips the choice.
- **Your call:** hold the slot or release it?
