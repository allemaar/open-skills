# Status [need:status]

Spine: current state; completed work that still matters; work in progress;
remaining work; blockers. Update at start, material transition, missed ETA,
blocker, and completion; otherwise report only material change. A progress bar
requires a real denominator.

Example:

**Kitchen renovation: `3/6` stages complete.**

- **Done:** demolition, plumbing, electrics.
- **Now:** cabinets; due Thursday.
- **Not yet:** counters and painting.
- **Needs you:** tile choice by Friday, or completion slips one week.
