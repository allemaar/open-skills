# Handover [need:handover]

Spine: current position; completed work with evidence; unfinished work; traps;
next action. The successor must not need hidden history to act safely.

Example:

**Event handover:**

- **Done:** venue paid; invitations sent; `24/42` confirmed.
- **Open:** final headcount due August 20.
- **Trap:** the sound system requires an adapter the band lacks.
- **Next step:** chase the `18` unanswered invitations before August 20.

Receipts and the guest list contain evidence, not a different status.
