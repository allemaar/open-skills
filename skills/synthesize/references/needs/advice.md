# Advice [need:advice]

Spine: labeled stance; basis; uncertainty; counter-case; what would change it.
Do not manufacture a counter-case.

Example:

**My take:** choose the reversible trial. It tests the benefit for one month
without locking in the annual fee.

**Counter-case:** monthly pricing costs `€12` more. If the annual refund is
unconditional, the yearly plan becomes better.

**What changes my view:** confirmation of that refund term.
