## 3. Understand / learn — "explain this" [need:learn]

Spine: concept · why it matters · example or contrast · limit or misconception. No recommendation; no stance.

**Compound interest in one line: your savings earn interest, then that interest earns interest too.**

- `€1,000` at `5%` → `€1,050` after year one
- year two's `5%` applies to the whole `€1,050`, not just the original — so growth accelerates by itself, every year

**The catch** — debt compounds identically, against you; a small card balance grows the same accelerating way.

**You've got it when this makes sense** — doubling takes about `72 ÷ rate` years.
