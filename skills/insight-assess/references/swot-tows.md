# Optional SWOT / TOWS

Use to connect a strategic situation to a named decision, not to manufacture a strategy from four lists. SWOT means strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats; TOWS pairs these factors to generate options.

- Fix subject, objective and horizon. Separate internal strengths/weaknesses from external opportunities/threats. Internal/external is the distinction, not present/future; a wish is not an opportunity.
- Include only material factors with evidence or an explicit assumption label. Keep confidence in evidence separate from likelihood of an event. Empty quadrants are acceptable. Do not multiply arbitrary scores into an objective-looking posture.
- Pair supported factors: strengths with opportunities or threats; weaknesses with opportunities or threats. Name the specific factors behind each proposed action and why the pairing could work. Do not force an action into every pairing.
- Compare feasible options against constraints and alternatives. Return the supported factors, traceable options, proposed next move and uncertainties. Suggest an owner, horizon and success measure only where justified; do not assign or execute work. A SWOT does not validate demand or investor claims.

Feed the options and evidence into the existing assessment verdict.

[Original TOWS method](https://doi.org/10.1016/0024-6301(82)90120-0). [Community implementation inspected](https://github.com/JayRHa/agent-skills/tree/f9a456bf34c297bd23fb234f410ec9b239ebed14/swot-analyzer).
