# Counsel, verification, and independent challenge

Status: **active**.

Choose the smallest challenge operation that changes the decision:

| Need | Operation |
|---|---|
| Advice before or during work | Driver–Counsel |
| Independent checking after an artifact exists | Driver–Verifier |
| Protection from peer anchoring | Blind Deposit |
| Fresh scrutiny after agreement | Break the Convergence |

For every operation:

1. Freeze or identify the exact question, artifact, bytes, and decision boundary.
2. Name the participant's role and whether it holds a pen. Reviewers do not edit the reviewed artifact.
3. Give acceptance criteria and relevant sources; withhold peer conclusions when independence requires it.
4. Require direct evidence, recomputation, or a first deposit appropriate to the operation.
5. Return findings with severity, evidence, uncertainty, and the smallest decision-changing repair.
6. Stop after the bounded review. Do not start another lens on unchanged evidence.

If review changes the artifact, refreeze it before relying on the result. A review structure without challenge behavior is theatre.
