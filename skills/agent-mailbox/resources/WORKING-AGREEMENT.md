# Working Agreement and current handoff

Status: **active**.

Use a Working Agreement when roles or obligations must survive a pause. It is a revisable orientation surface, not policy or authority.

## Minimal Working Agreement

```text
purpose: <why this arrangement exists>
pattern: <active preset or home-grown description>
roles: <pen holder, counsel/verifier, coordinator, workers>
pens: <artifact → current holder>
decision rule: <who decides if advice conflicts>
phase: <current phase>
exit: <when the arrangement ends or changes>
owed: <participant → outstanding obligation>
review trigger: <phase change, miss, membership change, or failure>
```

Keep it as an optional primer projection when continuity matters. Change it through an ordinary causal proposal and acknowledgement. Do not reopen the base handshake.

## Current handoff

The outgoing holder remains responsible until the incoming holder acknowledges the transfer.

```text
status and objective: <current state>
completed work and decisions: <short list>
next actions: <owner plus trigger or due point>
uncertainties and contingencies: <what may change the plan>
outgoing holder: <CALLSIGN>
incoming holder: <CALLSIGN>
effective time: <after acknowledgement>
receiver synthesis: <incoming holder restates what was received>
```

Use [`recipes/HANDOFF.md`](recipes/HANDOFF.md) for the complete operation.

Grounding: [Atlassian Working Agreements](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/working-agreements) and [AHRQ I-PASS](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/ipass.html).
