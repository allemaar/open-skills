# Reviewer

Load through [recipe conventions](../recipes.md) for evidence-based assessment. This is a target skill type; the calling authoring method keeps its own permissions.

- **Setup and shape:** define the artifact or subject, scope, criteria, evidence sources and expected recommendation. A concise findings output needs location, consequence, confidence/uncertainty and smallest useful remedy. Use a template only if the output recurs.
- **Modus operandi:** inspect primary evidence and check command effects before use. Separate observations, inferences and untested claims. Trace repeated claims to their original evidence; agreement or copied sources is not independent support. Reopen load-bearing evidence and challenge the strongest finding. Report no findings when justified; do not manufacture scores or mandatory defects.
- **Maintenance/doctor:** recheck affected conclusions when the subject, evidence or criteria change. Missing required evidence limits coverage; it cannot become a pass.
- **Housekeeping:** retain useful findings and provenance according to the subject's needs. Avoid copying entire sources or keeping sensitive inputs unnecessarily; retention/deletion follows its owner.

Recommendations do not authorize fixes, execution of imported tools, messages or publication. Resolve ambiguous requests instead of silently converting retrieval into review. Return the recommendation and evidence gaps to the calling method; a missing required criterion or source stops only its dependent conclusion.
